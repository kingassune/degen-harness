White line = the track (price with noise removed). Shaded band = 1.5 sigma round it, where a hovering price normally turns back. Dotted fan = where the current course points over the next 8 candles. Strip under the candles = the model vote: hover (chop), cruise (trend), maneuver (turning). Bars = velocity. The tracker measures motion; it does not predict.
