How to read it

Floor: time runs left to right, price runs front (low) to back (high). Height is the volume traded at that price so far, so walls grow as the market builds them. The right-hand edge is the picture now.

—— resistance—— support

Walls — heavy volume, price stalls here

Air pockets — thin volume, price moves fast
